03 The problem, through the founder story

A diagnosis is hard enough.
Then comes the paperwork.

Expert wording, codes nobody explains and bills you can't check. That is how a health problem becomes a money problem.

A Friday call, a four-day weekend

My wife and I were driving home from Space Camp on a Friday afternoon, with a car full of kids, when we got the call: her breast biopsy was positive for cancer. We were told to call her doctor on Tuesday.

For four days we searched the internet for answers. We pieced together fragments of medical information, but nothing told us what the diagnosis would do to us financially.

The system holds the records, the codes, the rules and the leverage. Patients and families navigate the pressure in the dark. Unbill was built to shift that balance.

Brian Pearson, founder

11 FAQ · no link

Frequently asked questions

01What is Unbill and how can it help me?

Draft: An AI healthcare advocate that reviews your bills against your plan and EOB, explains what you owe and helps you dispute what you don't.

02How is Unbill different from medical bill negotiation services?

Draft: Negotiators usually take 20 to 35% of what they save you. Unbill charges a flat membership, so you keep 100%.

03Can Unbill manage healthcare disputes for my whole family?

Draft: Yes. One household membership, with a separate private profile and cases for each person.

04What kinds of documents can I upload?

Draft: Bills, itemized bills, EOBs, plan documents, denial letters and collection notices.

05Why does Unbill cross-reference several records instead of just my bill?

Draft: A bill alone doesn't show what insurance paid or should have paid. Errors show up when the bill, the EOB and your plan disagree.

06How is Unbill different from ChatGPT Health?

Draft: Answer to confirm with Brian.

07What happens when an error or discrepancy is flagged?

Draft: Unbill explains the issue, cites the evidence and prepares the next step: a request, a dispute or an appeal.

08What billing discrepancies does Unbill detect?

Draft: Duplicate charges, upcoding, wrong codes, charges your plan should have covered and surprise out-of-network bills.

09How does Unbill protect my medical data?

Draft: Health records go through a protected, HIPAA-eligible AI path covered by a Business Associate Agreement. Our SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress; see our Trust Center for our security practices and audit status. [Encryption detail to confirm with Brian.]

10How much does Unbill cost?

Draft: Households from $135 a year. Employers from $3 per employee per month. No success fee.

11I want to partner with Unbill or offer it to my members. How do I get in touch?

Draft: Employers and brokers can request a quote or partner terms on Add Unbill. For anything else, use the contact form.
